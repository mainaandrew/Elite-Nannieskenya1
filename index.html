Questions parents ask

Are your nannies vetted?

Yes. Every nanny is ID verified, background-checked and reference-checked. They are also trained in childcare, first aid, cooking and housekeeping.

What if I don't like my nanny?

Tell us. We replace her for free so you always end up with a nanny you are happy with.

How fast can you find me a nanny?

We match you with 3 vetted nannies within 24–48 hours, anywhere in Kenya.

Do you serve my town?

Very likely. We serve Nairobi, Mombasa, Watamu, Malindi, Diani, Kilifi, Kisumu, Nakuru, Eldoret, Thika, Kiambu, Machakos and everywhere else in Kenya. Just call us.

How do I pay?

Pay via M-Pesa, Cash or Card. Prices are shown in both Ksh and USD.

What are your working hours?

Our nannies work 8:00am – 5:30pm, Monday to Saturday. Extra hours after 5:30pm are Ksh 500 / $4 per hour. Our office is open the same hours.

What is included in the Ksh 3,000 / $23 day?

A full day of care from a vetted, trained nanny from 8:00am to 5:30pm. There are no hidden fees. You only pay extra if you use extra hours.
